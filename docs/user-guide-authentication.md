## Creating an Account

### Email Registration

1. Navigate to the Sign Up page by clicking "Sign Up" in the navigation menu or on the login page.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Create a strong password that meets the following requirements:
   - At least 8 characters long
   - Contains at least one uppercase letter
   - Contains at least one lowercase letter
   - Contains at least one number
   - Contains at least one special character
4. Enter your first name and last name (optional).
5. Click the "Sign Up" button.
6. Check your email for a verification link.
7. Click the verification link to activate your account.
8. Once your account is activated, you will be redirected to the login page.

### Tips for Creating a Secure Account

- Use a unique email address that you have access to.
- Create a strong, unique password that you don't use for other accounts.
- Consider using a password manager to generate and store secure passwords.
- Keep your verification email for future reference.

## Logging In

### Standard Login

1. Navigate to the Login page.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your password in the Password field.
4. Click the "Login" button.
5. If you have multi-factor authentication enabled, you will be prompted to enter a verification code.
6. Once authenticated, you will be redirected to the dashboard.

### Staying Logged In

- Check the "Remember Me" option to stay logged in for 30 days.
- For security reasons, you will be automatically logged out after 30 days of inactivity.
- You can manually log out at any time by clicking "Logout" in the user menu.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

### What is Multi-Factor Authentication?

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) adds an extra layer of security to your account by requiring a second form of verification in addition to your password. This helps protect your account even if your password is compromised.

### Enabling MFA

1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to Account Settings.
3. Go to the "Security" section.
4. Click "Enable Multi-Factor Authentication".
5. Choose your preferred MFA method:
   - Authenticator App (recommended)
   - SMS
6. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your chosen method.
7. Once set up, you will be provided with backup codes. Store these in a safe place.
8. MFA is now enabled for your account.

### Using Authenticator App

1. Download and install an authenticator app on your mobile device:
   - Google Authenticator
   - Microsoft Authenticator
   - Authy
2. During MFA setup, scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to verify setup.
4. When logging in, you will be prompted to enter the current code from your authenticator app.

### Using Backup Codes

1. If you cannot access your primary MFA method, you can use a backup code.
2. On the login page, after entering your password, click "Use Backup Code".
3. Enter one of your backup codes.
4. Each backup code can only be used once.
5. After using a backup code, you should generate new backup codes in your account settings.

### Disabling MFA

1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to Account Settings.
3. Go to the "Security" section.
4. Click "Disable Multi-Factor Authentication".
5. Verify your identity by entering your password and current MFA code.
6. MFA will be disabled for your account.

## Password Management

### Changing Your Password

1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to Account Settings.
3. Go to the "Security" section.
4. Click "Change Password".
5. Enter your current password.
6. Enter your new password and confirm it.
7. Click "Save Changes".

### Forgot Password

1. Navigate to the Login page.
2. Click "Forgot Password".
3. Enter your email address.
4. Click "Send Reset Link".
5. Check your email for a password reset link.
6. Click the reset link in the email.
7. Enter your new password and confirm it.
8. Click "Reset Password".
9. You will be redirected to the login page where you can log in with your new password.

### Password Requirements

For security reasons, your password must meet the following requirements:

- At least 8 characters long
- Contains at least one uppercase letter
- Contains at least one lowercase letter
- Contains at least one number
- Contains at least one special character

## Troubleshooting

### Common Login Issues

#### I can't log in with my email and password

1. Make sure you're using the correct email address.
2. Check that you're entering the correct password.
3. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" feature.
4. Make sure your account has been activated by clicking the verification link in your registration email.

#### I'm not receiving the verification email

1. Check your spam or junk folder.
2. Make sure you entered the correct email address.
3. Add our email domain to your safe senders list.
4. If you still don't receive the email, contact support.

#### I'm locked out of my account

1. If you've made too many failed login attempts, your account may be temporarily locked.
2. Wait 30 minutes and try again.
3. If you still can't log in, contact support.

#### I can't access my authenticator app

1. Use one of your backup codes to log in.
2. If you don't have backup codes, contact support.
